Donate

If you find my work useful and would like to show your appreciation, you are welcome to make a donation. However, donations are never required or expected.

A donation is simply a thank-you for work already done. It does not purchase or guarantee any future work.

Please take care of yourself and your family first. If donating would meaningfully affect your budget, please don't donate. Your well-being matters more.
